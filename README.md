The one sentence a house cat gives in the five seconds after knocking a
glass off the table, in the cat's own voice. No apology, no confession —
just the most convincing case a cat can make for itself.

## Entry

> The glass was sitting in my sunbeam, and I am legally the sunbeam's
> owner, so the glass was trespassing and I evicted it.

121 characters, one sentence. The glass trespassed on the cat's sunbeam;
the cat evicted it. Cat logic, total lack of remorse, specific detail
(the sunbeam), and a fresh angle — no gravity, no slippery paw.

## How it works

`main.py` (stdlib only) validates the line — one sentence, 1–200
characters, no leading/trailing whitespace, single line — and writes it
to `/output/entry.txt`.

```sh
python3 main.py /output
```
